Child safety at Linkup

Linkup prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Last updated: 4 October 2026

What Linkup prohibits

You may not use Linkup to create, request, post, share, or distribute CSAM; groom or solicit a minor for sexual contact or sexual content; arrange or promote the sexual exploitation or trafficking of a child; or sexualize or endanger a minor.

These rules apply to plans, asks, chat messages, display names, group names, and other content or activity on Linkup. Users must be at least 13. Accounts aged 13 to 17 have limits on adults-only plans and Nightlife and After content.

Report a concern in the app

Signed-in Linkup users can submit reports. Choose Illegal activity or Unsafe or threatening when it fits, and add details that help explain the concern.

  • Open a plan and tap Report plan.
  • In Plans, find an ask and tap its Report ask flag.
  • In a plan chat, press and hold another person's message to report it.

For a concern about a person, or if you cannot use an in-app report, email dmt2014.business@gmail.com with enough information to identify the concern. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, or copies of suspected CSAM by email.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

How reports can be handled

In-app reports enter a moderator-only queue. Authorized moderators can review the reported content, hide plans, asks, or messages, suspend accounts, and record a report decision. Blocking another user is also available in the app.

Linkup may remove content or restrict an account when it violates these standards. The publisher is responsible for meeting applicable child-safety and legal reporting duties.
