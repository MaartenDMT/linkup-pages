Your account, your choice

Delete your Linkup account

You can delete your account in Linkup. If you cannot sign in or do not have the app, you can start an email request.

Delete from the app

  1. Open Linkup and sign in with the Google login linked to your account.
  2. Open You → Delete account.
  3. If Linkup asks, verify the linked Google login again.
  4. Type DELETE, then tap Delete my account to submit the deletion.

Deletion cannot be undone. Plans you host are removed for everyone.

If you cannot sign in or use the app

Email dmt2014.business@gmail.com with the subject Linkup account deletion. Include the email address or sign-in method associated with your Linkup account and your Linkup display name if you know it.

The publisher will verify that you control the account before processing the deletion request. Verification must not require you to send a password, one-time code, or OAuth sign-in code. Sending an email starts a request; it does not itself confirm that the account has been deleted.

What deletion removes

Account deletion removes your account and linked sign-in records and sessions, profile and birthday, plans, asks, messages, friends, groups, Tonight Mode sessions, notifications, push tokens, and account-linked analytics events. Plans you host are removed for everyone.

Safety report snapshots may remain after your account is deleted so moderators can handle reports and safety cases. These snapshots currently have no automatic expiry. Read the privacy policy for other retention details.
